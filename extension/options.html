Library access

Paper Fetch downloads with the access this browser already has. On campus or on your university VPN it needs no setup. For off-campus use, add your library's proxy below. It never sees or stores your password.

Your library's EZproxy sign-in link, the one that ends in url=. It usually looks like login.ezproxy.youruni.edu/login?url= or youruni.idm.oclc.org/login?url=. You can paste any link from your library's site that goes through the proxy and it is trimmed for you. Leave blank if you have none.

Only if your library subscribes to EBSCO. It is the part after /c/ in research.ebsco.com/c/…/ once you are signed in; leave it blank and it is looked up the first time EBSCO is needed.
