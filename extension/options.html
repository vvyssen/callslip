Library access

Paper Fetch downloads with the access this browser already has. On campus or on your university VPN it needs no setup. For off-campus use, add your library's proxy below. It never sees or stores your password.

    Pick your university and its proxy link is filled in. Not listed? Paste the link yourself: it is your library's EZproxy sign-in address, ending in url=, and any library link that goes through the proxy will do. Then press Sign in. The first time, Chrome asks whether Paper Fetch may read your library proxy's pages; that is how this page can tell when you are signed in.
